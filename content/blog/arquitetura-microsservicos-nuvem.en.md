## Building Resilient Cloud Applications

As businesses scale, monolithic codebases often become complex to maintain and deploy. Transitioning to well-architected microservices allows teams to build, test, and ship features independently and reliably.

### Essential Architectural Strategies

- **Service Decoupling:** Each microservice maintains its distinct business domain and isolated data store.
- **Containerization with Docker & Kubernetes:** Standardization across local, staging, and production environments.
- **Real-Time Observability:** Centralized logging, distributed metrics, and request tracing to detect performance bottlenecks immediately.
