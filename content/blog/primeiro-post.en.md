## The Importance of Application & Network Security

In today's digital landscape, application security and software resilience are crucial for companies of all sizes. Fast-growing businesses are frequent targets of automated threats when relying on un-audited or legacy software systems.

At **GS Edge**, we believe software technology should empower your organization with confidence and transparency.

### 3 Essential Steps for Secure Applications

1. **Robust Authentication & Authorization:** Implementing JWT, OAuth2, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all entry points.
2. **API Protection & Rate Limiting:** Protect your endpoints against brute-force attacks and DDoS using modern API Gateways and web application firewalls.
3. **Automated Testing & Dependency Audits:** Keep software libraries updated and execute continuous vulnerability testing within your CI/CD pipeline.

> "Prevention and well-tested code are the single best investment for sustainable technological growth."
